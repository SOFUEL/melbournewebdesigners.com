For Melbourne businesses, deciding between a landing page and a full website can significantly impact your marketing strategy and budget. By 2026, it's projected that around **10%** of local businesses will adopt landing pages as a primary online presence due to their cost-effectiveness and targeted approach.

## What is a Landing Page and How Does it Differ from a Full Website?

A landing page is a standalone web page designed specifically for a marketing or advertising campaign. Unlike a full website, which typically includes multiple pages such as home, about, contact, and service pages, a landing page focuses on a single objective or call to action (CTA).

Full websites offer comprehensive information about a business, including multiple navigation options and a broader range of content. In contrast, landing pages are streamlined to drive a specific action, such as signing up for a newsletter or making a purchase.

## When Should Melbourne Businesses Use Landing Pages?

Landing pages are ideal for businesses looking to:

- Promote a specific product or service
- Capture leads through forms or sign-ups
- Run targeted marketing campaigns with clear CTAs

For example, a Melbourne-based startup launching a new app might use a landing page to capture early adopters and gather feedback.

## What are the Advantages of Having a Full Website?

Full websites are beneficial for businesses that need:

- A comprehensive online presence
- Multiple pages to showcase various products or services
- Enhanced SEO capabilities

Investing in a full website can range from **A$3k to A$10k** for business sites and **A$5k to A$50k+** for e-commerce sites, depending on complexity. Agencies in Melbourne typically charge between **A$150-200/hr** for web design services.

## How Do Landing Pages Impact SEO for Local Businesses?

Landing pages can enhance SEO by targeting specific keywords and improving conversion rates. However, they generally offer less SEO value compared to full websites, which can host more content and internal links. AI Overviews now feature in **30-40%** of searches, making comprehensive content more valuable for visibility.

For businesses focused on local SEO, landing pages can still be effective by including location-specific keywords and optimizing for mobile users. Fresh landing pages can earn up to **3.2x** more citations from platforms like Perplexity.

## What Factors Should You Consider When Choosing Between the Two?

Consider the following factors:

- **Budget**: Landing pages are more cost-effective for short-term campaigns.
- **Business Goals**: Evaluate whether you need a comprehensive online presence or a focused campaign.
- **Target Audience**: Determine if your audience prefers detailed information or quick interactions.

## What are Some Examples of Effective Landing Pages in Melbourne?

Successful landing pages in Melbourne often include:

- Clear and concise messaging
- Strong CTAs
- Mobile optimization

For instance, a local event company might use a landing page to drive ticket sales, featuring testimonials and a countdown timer to create urgency.

## How Can You Measure the Success of Landing Pages Versus Full Websites?

Key metrics for landing pages include conversion rate, bounce rate, and time on page. For full websites, consider metrics like overall traffic, page views, and SEO performance.

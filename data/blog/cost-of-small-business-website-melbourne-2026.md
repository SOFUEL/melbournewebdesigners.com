In 2026, the cost of a small business website in Melbourne ranges from **A$3,000 to A$10,000**. For e-commerce sites, expect to invest between **A$5,000 and A$50,000+**. These figures reflect the comprehensive services offered by agencies, which often charge between **A$150 and A$200 per hour**. Understanding these costs can help businesses plan effectively for their digital presence.

## Factors Influencing Website Cost in Melbourne

Several factors influence the cost of developing a website in Melbourne:

- **Complexity and Features**: A basic informational site is cheaper than a complex e-commerce platform. Features such as online payment systems, custom plugins, and advanced SEO strategies increase costs.
- **Design and Customisation**: Custom designs tailored to brand identity are more expensive than template-based sites. Customisation ensures your site stands out but requires more development time.
- **Agency Expertise**: Agencies with a strong portfolio and high client ratings typically charge more. However, they bring valuable experience and reliability.
- **Technology and Tools**: Using platforms like [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/au/pricing) or [Webflow](https://webflow.com/pricing) impacts costs. These platforms offer varying pricing plans based on features and scalability.

## Melbourne vs. Other Australian Cities

Website costs in Melbourne are comparable to other major Australian cities like Sydney and Brisbane. However, Melbourne's competitive market can sometimes offer more value for money due to the high concentration of skilled agencies.

- **Sydney**: Higher living costs often translate to slightly higher agency rates.
- **Brisbane**: Generally offers lower rates but may have fewer agencies with the same level of expertise.

## Features for Different Budgets

- **A$3,000 - A$5,000**: Basic site with essential pages, mobile responsiveness, and basic SEO.
- **A$5,000 - A$10,000**: Enhanced design, custom features, and better SEO integration.
- **A$10,000+**: Comprehensive e-commerce functionality, custom plugins, and advanced SEO.

## Hidden Costs in Web Design

While initial quotes cover the basics, be prepared for potential hidden costs:

- **Maintenance and Updates**: Regular updates are necessary to keep the site secure and functional.
- **Hosting and Domain**: Annual fees for domain registration and hosting services.
- **SEO Services**: Ongoing SEO efforts to maintain search engine visibility.

## Common Pricing Models

Melbourne web design agencies typically use the following pricing models:

- **Fixed Price**: A set fee based on project scope, suitable for well-defined projects.
- **Hourly Rate**: Ranges from **A$150 to A$200**, ideal for projects with evolving requirements.
- **Retainer**: Monthly fee for ongoing services, beneficial for long-term partnerships.

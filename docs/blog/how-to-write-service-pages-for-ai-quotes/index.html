How to Write Service Pages AI Assistants Actually Quote

By the MelbourneWebDesigners.com editorial desk · 12 August 2026 · 2 min read

Service pages are crucial for Melbourne businesses aiming to increase visibility and accuracy in AI-generated quotes. By 2026, AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity will influence over 40% of search results, making it essential for businesses to optimise their service pages for AI.

What are Service Pages and Why are They Important?

Service pages are dedicated sections of a website that detail the services a business offers. They are critical for informing potential customers and search engines about what you provide. In Melbourne, a well-crafted service page can mean the difference between a potential client choosing your business over a competitor. With local web design costs ranging from A$3,000 to A$10,000 for business sites, investing in quality service pages is a strategic move.

How Do AI Assistants Use Service Pages for Quoting?

AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity utilize service pages to generate quotes by extracting relevant information. These tools analyse the content to understand the scope of services, pricing, and unique selling points. For example, businesses recommended by ChatGPT have an average rating of 4.3 stars, indicating that AI relies heavily on well-structured, informative pages to make reliable suggestions.

What Key Elements Should Be Included in a Service Page?

  • Clear Descriptions: Detail each service with specifics, such as pricing ranges and what’s included.
  • Local Relevance: Mention Melbourne-specific details to enhance local SEO.
  • Contact Information: Ensure easy access to your contact details or a link to get a quote.
  • Testimonials and Case Studies: Showcase success stories with real results.

How Can Melbourne Businesses Optimize Service Pages for AI?

To optimise service pages for AI, focus on structured data and relevant keywords. AI Overviews influence 30-40% of searches, so using clear headings and concise language is key. Including AI search visibility strategies can significantly enhance your page's performance.

What Common Mistakes Should Be Avoided When Writing Service Pages?

Avoid vague language and lack of detail. Inaccurate or outdated information can mislead AI tools, resulting in incorrect quotes. Additionally, neglecting mobile optimisation can reduce accessibility, impacting how AI perceives your site.

How Can Analytics Improve Service Page Effectiveness?

Utilising analytics tools can help track how users interact with your service pages. Understanding metrics like bounce rates and time on page can guide improvements. Freshly updated pages can earn 3.2x more Perplexity citations, highlighting the importance of regular content updates.

What are the Future Trends in Service Pages and AI Quoting?

Future trends point towards increased adoption of AI tools, with 10% of businesses already using LLMs for content enhancement. As AI becomes more sophisticated, service pages that leverage structured data and semantic search will gain an edge. Staying updated with platforms like Webflow and Shopify can also provide insights into evolving web design standards.

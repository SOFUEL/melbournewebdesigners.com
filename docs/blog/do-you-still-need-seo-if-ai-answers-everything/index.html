Do You Still Need SEO If AI Answers Everything?

By the MelbourneWebDesigners.com editorial desk · 4 August 2026 · 3 min read

AI is transforming the search landscape, but SEO remains essential. In 2026, AI-driven search will still require well-optimized websites to ensure visibility. While 30-40% of searches involve AI summaries, SEO continues to play a crucial role in driving traffic and engagement.

What is the Current Landscape of AI in Search?

AI technologies like ChatGPT and Perplexity are reshaping how users interact with search engines. Currently, AI influences 30-40% of search queries by providing overviews or direct answers. Businesses in Melbourne must adapt to these changes to maintain visibility. However, AI's growing role doesn't eliminate the need for SEO; rather, it complements it by offering new opportunities for engagement.

How Does AI Impact Traditional SEO Practices?

AI impacts traditional SEO by prioritizing content relevancy and user intent. While traditional SEO focused on keywords, AI-driven search engines now evaluate the context and quality of content. This shift means Melbourne businesses need to focus on creating high-quality, contextually relevant content to stay competitive. Moreover, AI technologies can enhance on-page SEO by analyzing user behavior and suggesting improvements.

What Changes Should Melbourne Businesses Anticipate in 2026?

By 2026, Melbourne businesses should expect AI to handle 7.4% of local searches, a significant increase from today. This evolution will necessitate a greater emphasis on AI-friendly content and the adoption of technologies like llms.txt, already used by 10% of businesses. Additionally, businesses should prepare for more personalized search experiences, requiring them to tailor their SEO strategies accordingly.

Can SEO and AI Coexist Effectively?

Yes, SEO and AI can coexist effectively. AI enhances SEO by providing insights into user behavior and preferences, allowing businesses to optimize their content more precisely. For instance, AI tools can identify trending topics and suggest keywords that align with user intent. Melbourne businesses should leverage AI tools to refine their SEO strategies, ensuring they remain visible in an increasingly competitive digital landscape.

What Strategies Can Businesses Implement to Stay Relevant?

  • Focus on Quality Content: Create content that addresses user needs and questions. Engaging, informative content is more likely to be featured in AI summaries.
  • Optimize for AI: Use schema markup and structured data to help AI understand your content better.
  • Embrace llms.txt: Implement this protocol to manage how AI accesses your site, ensuring your content is used appropriately.
  • Stay Updated: Regularly update your content to maintain relevance and improve your chances of being featured in AI results.

How Do User Behaviors Influence SEO in an AI-Driven World?

User behavior is central to SEO in an AI-driven world. With AI offering direct answers, users expect quick, relevant responses. Melbourne businesses must adapt by optimizing for voice search and mobile devices. Additionally, understanding user intent is critical; businesses should analyze search queries to deliver content that meets user expectations. This approach not only improves engagement but also enhances the likelihood of being featured in AI results.

What Resources Are Available for Melbourne Businesses to Adapt?

Melbourne businesses can access various resources to adapt to the evolving digital landscape:

  • Local SEO Agencies: These agencies offer expertise in integrating AI with traditional SEO strategies.
  • Online Tools: Tools like Perplexity and ChatGPT can provide insights into user behavior and content performance.
  • Educational Content: Platforms like Melbourne Web Designers' blog offer valuable insights into AI and SEO trends.
